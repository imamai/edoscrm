## Processing

Each was fetched at 2000px wide and re-encoded with mozjpeg at quality 80.
Hero frames are 1800x1013 (16:9), capability frames 1040x780 (4:3), and the
auth panel 900x1400 (portrait). All were cropped, never squeezed.

Crops used sharp's attention strategy — it frames the most salient region —
**except** `hero-team.jpg` and `triage.jpg`, which it got badly wrong: it
chose an empty window and a blank wall respectively, cropping the people
straight out of both. Those two are centre crops. Look at the result before
trusting the strategy; it is right most of the time and confidently wrong the
rest.

## Kenyan setting

The brief was a local setting, and the first pass was not local enough. The
frames that carry a face — intake, triage, insight, the hero and the sign-in
panel — were re-sourced so the people in them look like the people who will
use this. `root-cause.jpg` is the one deliberate exception: it shows two
people working a problem out at a whiteboard, which is the activity the
section is about, and no African-set equivalent in the library showed the
work rather than a portrait.

Searching "african <thing>" returns a pool of African *people* rather than
African *workplaces* — the same handful of portraits come back for
"warehouse", "engineer" and "call centre" alike. Searching for the work
itself is what found the pictures of the job being done.

Every frame was reviewed at full size for legible third-party branding and
for anything that would date the picture. Two otherwise good candidates were
dropped for exactly that: a boardroom full of face masks, and a card held up
to camera with a word printed on it.
